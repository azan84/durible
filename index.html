Ordo Lab · Durible™

Printing with what Malaysia throws away.

Durible™ is our materials research: bioplastics made from durian husk, one of Malaysia's most abundant agricultural by-products. Instead of letting the husks go to landfill, we're turning them into feedstock for locally made 3D printing material.

It started on a small bench at IIUM's Kulliyyah of Engineering, printing keepsakes for alumni. The goal now is a printer and a material that are both born in Malaysia.

100% locally sourced Zero petroleum plastics Prototype phase

  3. 3
